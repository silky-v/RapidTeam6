HOW AN ENGAGEMENT WORKS

Clear scope, controlled testing and findings your team can act on.

01 / SCOPE

Plan the assessment

We discuss your goals, systems, constraints and timing, then document the assets in scope, exclusions, contacts and written authorisation.

02 / TEST

Assess safely

Testing follows the agreed rules of engagement, with checks to limit disruption and protect data. We pause and contact you if an unexpected risk appears.

03 / REPORT

Explain the findings

We validate issues and describe their evidence, likely impact, severity and practical remediation options in a clear report.

04 / IMPROVE

Support remediation

We review questions with your team and can agree a follow-up retest to check whether fixes address the reported issues.

WHAT YOU RECEIVE

Each engagement is tailored to the agreed service and scope.

Written scope

Agreed assets, test window, exclusions, contacts, safety limits and authorisation before testing begins.

Prioritised findings

A plain-language summary plus technical evidence, impact, severity and remediation guidance for validated issues.

Readout and next steps

A findings discussion with your team, answers to remediation questions and optional retesting by agreement.

Observation: During a hypothetical authorised review, recovery settings allowed an outdated secondary contact to remain active.

ABOUT RAPID TEAM 6

Responsible security testing with a defensive mindset.

TEST. FIND. IMPROVE.

Rapid Team 6 is a three-person computer security testing team focused on authorised assessments and responsible research. Our goal is to find security issues before they become serious problems and help make technology safer.

Jason

SECURITY RESEARCHER

Jason investigates how software behaves at its boundaries: how applications handle unexpected input, connect services, manage sessions and enforce access rules. His research has included finding bugs affecting Facebook, Instagram and Snapchat.

He works to turn an observed failure into a clear, reproducible security finding: documenting the affected component, the conditions needed to trigger the issue, its realistic impact and the systems or data that could be at risk. That helps owners distinguish a cosmetic defect from a weakness that needs urgent attention.

For authorised client work, Jason helps define test scope, validates findings carefully, avoids unnecessary access to data and prepares practical remediation guidance so teams can verify that a fix addresses the root cause.

Andy

RED TEAMER

Andy plans controlled red team exercises to assess how well an organisation can prevent, detect and respond to realistic attack paths. The work begins with agreed objectives, written permission, boundaries, safety checks and clear stop conditions.

Within that scope, he maps plausible routes through exposed services, identity controls, endpoints and business processes, then emulates relevant adversary techniques in a measured way. The focus is on testing defensive assumptions and detection coverage, not causing disruption or accessing information beyond what the exercise requires.

After an exercise, Andy reconstructs the sequence of events, identifies where safeguards worked or failed, and works with defenders on prioritized improvements to monitoring, access controls, response playbooks and recovery procedures.

Steve

FORMER FACEBOOK SECURITY ENGINEER

Steve brings experience from security engineering at Facebook, where security work at platform scale depends on designing controls that remain dependable across complex services, teams and changing threats.

He applies that engineering mindset to threat modelling, secure architecture and practical control reviews: tracing sensitive data flows, examining trust boundaries, checking how identity and permissions are enforced, and considering how systems behave when a component or account is compromised.

Steve helps translate assessment results into durable engineering changes. He works with teams to prioritize fixes by risk, strengthen layered safeguards, improve security visibility and make incident response and recovery more reliable.
